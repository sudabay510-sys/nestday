# Nestday — Brand identity

**Slug:** `nestday` · **Tagline:** Gentle day-rhythm and bedtime-routine ideas for new parents — education, not medical advice.
**Status:** Early access. Checkout not live; founding list only. No payments processed.

## Positioning
Tired parents and caregivers of babies and toddlers (0–3) who want a simple, flexible daily scaffold.

**Promise (honest):** General education only. Not medical advice. No sleep-outcome promises.

## Personas
- **First-time parent:** Every day feels different; you’d like a loose shape.
- **Back-to-work parent:** Daycare drop-off, evenings, and a bedtime that works for everyone.
- **Tag-team caregivers:** Partners or grandparents who need the same playbook.

## Customer journey the landing page follows
1. **Survive** — pain: Days blur together. → plan: Age-based rhythm ideas — flexible, not rigid.
2. **Wind down** — pain: Bedtime is a battle. → plan: A short, repeatable wind-down checklist.
3. **Share the load** — pain: Only one person knows the routine. → plan: A one-page caregiver hand-off sheet.
4. **Ask for help** — pain: Not sure what’s normal. → plan: Clear pointers to call your pediatrician.

## Voice & tone
Soft, reassuring, never judgmental. Short sentences for tired eyes.
- Short, concrete sentences. Checklists over hype.
- Never shame, scare, or invent urgency.

## Safety / compliance line
Nestday is not medical care. For fever, breathing trouble, feeding or growth worries, or anything that concerns you, contact your pediatrician. In an emergency, call 911.

## Do
- Lead with reassurance and safety.
- Point to the pediatrician and safe-sleep guidance.
- Show intro **and** renewal prices before any future charge.
- Label the founding list as “no charge today”.
- Keep the disclaimer visible near every plan.

## Don’t
- Diagnose, give dosing/feeding medical advice, or mandate sleep-training methods.
- Promise sleeping through the night or any outcome.
- Fake testimonials, user counts, stats, or press logos.
- Countdown timers, “spots left”, or other urgency/scare copy.
